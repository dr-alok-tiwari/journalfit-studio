# Responsible-Use Statement

JournalFit Studio is a journal discovery and fit-assessment assistant. It is intended to help researchers shortlist journals that may be suitable for a manuscript based on the manuscript title, abstract, keywords, research area, and local journal metadata.

## Core principles

1. **Journal fit is not journal acceptance.** A recommendation does not imply that a journal will accept, review, or prioritise the manuscript.
2. **Ranking fields are optional context.** If a workbook contains ranking-related data, the app treats such information as supplementary context only.
3. **Official verification is mandatory.** Researchers must verify aims and scope, author guidelines, fees, indexing, ethics policy, article type, and submission route on the official journal website.
4. **No mechanical use.** The tool should not be used mechanically for hiring, promotion, performance review, incentives, or publication-quality judgement.
5. **Local metadata must be maintained responsibly.** Outdated or incomplete metadata can lead to weak or misleading results.
6. **Confidential manuscripts require appropriate deployment safeguards.** Local use is preferred for sensitive work. Hosted deployments should provide a clear privacy and retention policy.

## Recommended disclaimer for users

Journal-fit suggestions are decision-support outputs. They are based on the metadata available to the app at the time of use. The researcher remains responsible for checking official journal information and making the final submission decision.

## Verification checklist

Before submitting to any shortlisted journal, verify:

- Official aims and scope.
- Accepted article types.
- Word limits and formatting rules.
- Submission portal.
- APCs or publication charges.
- Indexing and abstracting status.
- Review model and publication ethics policy.
- Recent articles in the journal.
- Editorial board and publisher information.
- Any institutional or funder requirements.
