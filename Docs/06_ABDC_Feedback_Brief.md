# Feedback Brief for External Reviewers

## Purpose of this brief

This brief summarises the type of feedback requested from external reviewers, including academic councils, journal-quality stakeholders, research-governance experts, and institutional research offices.

## Framing

JournalFit Studio is a journal discovery and fit assistant. Its primary purpose is to identify potentially suitable journals based on manuscript content and journal metadata. It is not intended to promote ranking-driven publication decisions.

Optional ranking-related fields may be present in user-provided metadata, but they are treated as contextual information. The central recommendation logic is based on title, abstract, keywords, field, scope, topics, and metadata-richness signals.

## Feedback requested

Reviewers are invited to comment on:

1. Whether the journal-discovery framing is appropriate.
2. Whether the distinction between journal suitability and ranking context is sufficiently clear.
3. Whether any wording could encourage overreliance on rankings.
4. Whether the disclaimers are visible and sufficiently cautious.
5. Whether the verification checklist is adequate.
6. Whether the tool should include additional metadata fields before wider use.
7. Whether the hosted demo requires stronger privacy language.
8. Whether the contributor attribution and non-affiliation statement are appropriate.
9. Whether a tool of this kind can be responsibly improved for broader academic use.

## Non-endorsement clarification

Sharing the tool for review does not imply endorsement, certification, validation, or official association by any external reviewer. The purpose is to seek constructive guidance for responsible improvement.

## Suggested feedback format

Reviewers may provide feedback under three headings:

- Language and positioning.
- Technical and data-quality improvements.
- Governance and responsible-use safeguards.
